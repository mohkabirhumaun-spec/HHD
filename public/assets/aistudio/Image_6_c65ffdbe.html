Biomass & Urban Winter Particulate Strategy

Rural women face extensive indoor biomass smoke from traditional clay stoves (chulha), causing non-smoker COPD. In urban centres like Dhaka and Narayanganj, winter PM2.5 levels exceed safe limits 8-fold. Use well-fitted masks during November–February transit and cross-ventilate kitchen zones.
