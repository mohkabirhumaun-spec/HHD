## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, scientifically rigorous, and deeply human visual framework. Designed primarily for high-stakes public health, genetic intelligence, and population-level human development initiatives across the Global South, the system blends institutional confidence with inclusive clarity.

The aesthetic philosophy adheres to **Corporate / Modern** principles infused with clinical precision:
- **Tone & Demeanor:** Trustworthy, objective, empathetic, and forward-looking. Visuals avoid cold clinical sterility while maintaining unwavering epidemiological rigor.
- **Audience:** Healthcare professionals, genetic researchers, community health workers, policy makers, and individuals across multilingual contexts (English and Bengali).
- **Visual Tenets:** Generous whitespace, precise micro-borders, clear structural hierarchy, and functional semantic color coding to distinguish between clinical genetics, preventative wellness, technology analytics, and urgent public alerts.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to communicate credibility while enabling multi-domain clinical and research categorizations.

- **Primary (`#102A43` - Deep Navy):** Anchors navigation bars, institutional banners, primary actions, and primary typography. Represents stability and structural integrity.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - Health Teal):** Represents proactive prevention, clinical education, wellness screenings, and public health campaigns.
- **Tertiary (`#7C3AED` - Vibrant Violet):** Dedicated to specialized genetic health, genomics registries, hemoglobinopathy tracking, and scientific innovation.
- **Supporting Accents & Functional Indicators:**
  - **Electric Blue (`#3B82F6`):** Applied to telemetry, machine learning models, predictive intelligence, and system analytics.
  - **Coral (`#F97373`):** High-priority alerts, community mobilization initiatives, and carrier awareness drives.
  - **Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Statistical notices, observational metrics, and clinical caution flags.
  - **Emerald (`#10B981`):** Verified carrier screens, completed clinical protocols, and validated outcomes.
- **Canvas & Surface Base:** Background surfaces utilize clean neutrals (`#F7FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`), paired with `#FFFFFF` card layers and deep slate (`#1E293B`) body copy to maintain WCAG AAA contrast ratios.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and structural titles with **Inter** for clinical data, tabular findings, and extended narrative sections.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides modern, geometric warmth with distinct counters that remain legible in prominent titles and analytical cards.
- **Inter:** Ensures high legibility across dense data tables, medical research summaries, and multilingual script interfaces.
- **Bilingual Typographic Balancing:** When rendering parallel English and Bengali (à¦¬à¦¾à¦‚à¦²à¦¾) text:
  - Bengali scripts display naturally taller ascenders and descenders. Increase relative line height by 15% to 20% on all body text when rendering Bengali content.
  - Maintain identical optical weights between Latin and Bengali typefaces to preserve visual parity in side-by-side or stacked translations.
