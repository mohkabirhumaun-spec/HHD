Why Hemoglobin Electrophoresis (HPLC) Matters

Standard Complete Blood Count (CBC) only flags low red cell volume (MCV < 80 fL), which frequently mimics iron deficiency. High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) is definitive, measuring HbA2 and identifying HbE variants accurately before marriage.

verified_user Empathetic Care Protocol

Voluntary • Privacy-Preserving Pathway

A structured, voluntary framework safeguarding autonomy and data protection at every stage.

1
Pre-Test Literacy & Consent

Bilingual educational counseling. Informed consent ensures zero social pressure and clarifies that both partners must test for any risk calculation.

2
CBC & Variant Analysis (HPLC)

Single 3-5 mL venous sample processed via accredited automated HPLC instrumentation for precise Hemoglobin A2, F, and abnormal variant quantification.

3
Confidential Genetic Counseling

One-on-one session with a certified clinical genetic counselor. Non-judgmental interpretation, pedigree risk charting, and reproductive options guidance.
